What DARA does

Seasonal edits, complete looks and a simple "shop the look" breakdown: a top, trousers and a bag, each linked on its own, with the price of every piece and the whole look.

How looks are chosen

  • Demand: retailer best-seller and trending signals, and UK search interest.
  • Quality: review sentiment, sizing and returns feedback.
  • Trust: an item is only featured if followers would not feel misled by it, whatever it earns.

Transparency

DARA is an AI-hosted character, not a real person. All content is human reviewed. Looks are AI-styled visualisations and show similar picks, not necessarily the exact garments. DARA has not physically tested the products.
